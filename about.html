Our Story

More Than 50 Years of Music

The Real Ozone Rangers have been making music in the Tri-Cities since 1975. For more than 50 years, the band has brought live music to local audiences at community events, festivals, farmers markets, private gatherings, and other venues throughout the region.

Our Music

The Real Ozone Rangers bring together a wide variety of musical influences and songs from across the years. Their performances include favorites from artists such as the Grateful Dead, The Beatles, Violent Femmes, and John Prine, along with other covers and original music.

Meet the Band

The Real Ozone Rangers are more than a group of musicians. Each member brings a unique background, personality, and experience to the band. Get to know the people behind the music.

Peter Montanti

Peter Montanti is retired and enjoying life making music with The Real Ozone Rangers. Outside of music, Peter worked as a commercial and architectural photographer, specializing primarily in architectural photography.

Marc Fagelson

Mark works at ETSU and describes himself as one of the newer members of The Real Ozone Rangers, although he has been playing with the group for close to 20 years. He enjoys living in the region and especially appreciates its welcoming character.

Jeffrey Hoare

Jeffrey Hoare has lived in the region for about 10 years and jokingly describes himself as a "recovering academic." He had wanted to be part of a garage band since he was a teenager, and with The Real Ozone Rangers, he finally found his band.

Steve Cook

Steve Cook has been making music with this group for around 40 years. For Steve, being an Ozone Ranger is about having a good time, entertaining people, and enjoying the company of a good group of friends. He also shared the band's memorable slogan: "Always welcome, never invited."

Davis Cunnin

Davis is a paleontologist and assistant collections manager at the Gray Fossil Site. Away from music and fossils, he has another unusual interest: keeping poison dart frogs.
