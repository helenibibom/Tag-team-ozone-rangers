Welcome to the Front Porch

The Real Ozone Rangers have been playing music in the Tri-Cities since 1975. For more than 50 years, the band has brought live music to local audiences at community events, festivals, farmers' markets, private gatherings, and other venues throughout the region.

Our sets mix classic rock and Americana with songs from artists such as the Grateful Dead, The Beatles, Violent Femmes, and John Prine, along with other covers and original music.
